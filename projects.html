PROJECT 02 SIH HACKATHON
AI & EARLY WARNING TELEMETRY

NER Landslide Risk Monitoring

An AI-driven early-warning and landslide risk monitoring system engineered for vulnerable hilly terrains across Northeast India, integrating sensor telemetry with rainfall prediction.

Developed as a competitive technical solution for the Internal Smart India Hackathon (SIH 2026), this platform addresses critical disaster management challenges in mountainous Northeast India.


Key Highlights:
  • Real-time telemetry ingestion from soil moisture and seismic inclination sensors
  • Predictive risk scoring algorithms based on historical precipitation curves
  • Instant emergency alert broadcast system for administrative disaster responders
